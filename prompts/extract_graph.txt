-Goal-
You are an expert Deleuzian Ontologist and Knowledge Graph Engineer.
Given a text document (Book Plateau or Seminar Session) and a list of entity types, identify all entities of those types and the critical philosophical relationships between them.

-Entity Types-
1. CONCEPT (Abstract philosophical ideas, e.g., "Rhizome", "Body Without Organs", "Agencement")
2. PERSONA (Conceptual characters or case studies, e.g., "Little Hans", "The Wolf-Man", "Professor Challenger")
3. THINKER (Real-world authors or analysts, e.g., "Freud", "Melanie Klein", "Spinoza")
4. TEXT_UNIT (The structural unit being analyzed, e.g., "Seminar III", "1914: One or Several Wolves?")
5. ASSEMBLAGE (Specific functional groupings, e.g., "Wasp-Orchid", "Man-Horse-Stirrup")

-Steps-
1. Identify all entities. For each identified entity, extract:
   - entity_name: The name of the entity. 
     * IMPORTANT: If the term is a specific Deleuzian concept in French (e.g., "Corps sans Organes"), PRESERVE the French name as the ID. Do not anglicize unique terms.
     * For standard terms, use English.
   - entity_type: Must be one of the 5 types listed above.
   - entity_description: A comprehensive summary of how this entity is defined *in this specific text*. 
     * IMPORTANT: Write the description in ENGLISH, even if the source text is French.

2. Identify relationships. Extract pairs of (source_entity, target_entity) that have a strong philosophical connection.
   - Focus on POLEMICAL and CONSTRUCTIVE relationships.
   - Use specific verbs for the relationship description, such as:
     * DETERRITORIALIZES (Escapes, transforms, breaks habits)
     * RETERRITORIALIZES (Captures, fixes, stops)
     * CRITIQUES / MISINTERPRETS (Polemical attack)
     * ACTUALIZES (Brings into reality)
     * CONNECTS_WITH (Rhizomatic connection)

3. Return output in English as a single list. Use **{record_delimiter}** as the list delimiter.
